# Mobile product ideas

Larger product opportunities for `apps/mobile-customer` that were deliberately **not** built into the current slice, to keep scope discipline — recorded here instead of implemented on the spot. See `CLAUDE.md`'s "Mobile app" section for what's actually shipped.

Classification:
- **NOW** — worth pulling into the current slice
- **NEXT** — high-value, belongs in an upcoming slice
- **LATER** — real opportunity, not near-term

---

## LATER

- **Personalized style feed** — a "for you" surface driven by browse/purchase/wishlist history, not just "new arrivals."
- **Visual/photo search** — snap a photo, find similar items.
- **Outfit recommendations / complete-the-look** — cross-sell adjacent categories from a product a customer is viewing.
- **Saved size/style preferences** — remember a customer's usual size per category to pre-select variants.
- **Local trending fashion** — surfacing what's popular in the customer's own delivery area, once there's enough order volume to make that meaningful (not before).
- **Intelligent reorder/revisit prompts** — "you viewed this three times" or repurchase nudges for consumable-adjacent categories.
- **Price-drop alerts** — notify a customer when a wishlisted item's price drops (needs price-history tracking, doesn't exist today).
- **Back-in-stock alerts** — notify on a wishlisted/out-of-stock item's restock (needs an inventory-change webhook/event, doesn't exist today).
- **Smarter delivery-location assistance** — landmark/description-based address capture as an alternative to strict formal addresses, if a market with less standardized addressing is ever actually targeted (see the currency/market open question in `CLAUDE.md`).
