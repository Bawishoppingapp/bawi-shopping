# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

Do not use Bawi Shopping to break the law; deceive, harass, exploit, or harm others; interfere with systems or accounts; submit false payment evidence; manipulate orders; or sell prohibited, stolen, counterfeit, unsafe, recalled, regulated, or rights-infringing goods. Sellers must provide truthful listings and have the right to sell every product offered.

We may investigate, remove content, restrict transactions, suspend accounts, preserve evidence, and cooperate with lawful authorities. Report concerns to **support@bawishopping.com**.
