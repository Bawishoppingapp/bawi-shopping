# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

Bawi Shopping is an online marketplace operated under the trading name **Bawi Shopping** from Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Contact us at **support@bawishopping.com**.

## Information we collect and use

We collect information needed to provide the marketplace: account and contact details, delivery addresses, order and support history, seller listings, payment-transfer references and receipt images, device and security logs, and courier delivery information. We use it to operate accounts, process and deliver orders, verify manual Telebirr payments, communicate updates, support users, prevent fraud, and meet applicable legal obligations. Passwords are securely hashed. We do not sell personal information.

## Providers, retention and security

Customers transfer payment to the Telebirr recipient shown at checkout and upload proof for administrator review. Do not upload unrelated financial information. We may give necessary information to hosting, email, storage, security, and delivery providers that help operate the service. We keep records only as reasonably needed for service, fraud prevention, disputes, and applicable recordkeeping duties. We use access controls, encrypted connections, tenant separation, and activity logs, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information, subject to lawful retention duties. Email **support@bawishopping.com** with the subject “Privacy Request.” The service is intended for people aged 18 or older.

We may update this policy and will revise the date above. Contact: **support@bawishopping.com**, Bawi Shopping, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.
