# Cancellations, Returns and Refunds

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

Customers may request cancellation before courier pickup. Returns are not generally accepted at launch. For an incorrect, damaged, defective, or materially misdescribed item, promptly email **support@bawishopping.com** with the order number and clear photos.

After an approved cancellation of a verified payment, Bawi Shopping confirms the original Telebirr transfer and manually returns the approved amount to the verified payer. Never request payment to a different number based only on a message or screenshot. Delivery fees already incurred may not be refundable unless Bawi Shopping, the seller, or courier caused the problem. Non-waivable consumer rights remain unaffected.
