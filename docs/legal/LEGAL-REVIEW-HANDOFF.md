# Legal Review Handoff

This is an organizational checklist, not legal advice.

## Counsel to retain

Use a licensed attorney who can advise on e-commerce, privacy/data protection, marketplaces, and payments in every launch jurisdiction. Because the product is intended for Ethiopia and the United States, ask whether one firm can cover both or whether Ethiopian and U.S. counsel should coordinate. Verify the lawyer's license directly with the applicable bar or professional regulator before sharing sensitive materials.

## Send counsel

- Every file in this `docs/legal/` directory
- The intended launch countries and any states/regions where customers or sellers will be accepted
- Registered company name, jurisdiction, address, support contacts, and trademark ownership
- A plain-language description of customer, seller, courier, and administrator roles
- The full data inventory: account/contact data, addresses, orders, wishlists, notifications, push tokens, seller identity/business records, product images, audit logs, and future payment references
- Every processor/subprocessor and hosting location once selected (hosting, database, storage, email, push, analytics, support, payment provider)
- Retention/deletion schedule, security controls, breach-response process, and cross-border data flows
- Commission, shipping, cancellation, returns, refunds, seller payout, prohibited-product, dispute, and courier policies
- The app's account-deletion behavior and which financial/order records must be retained by law
- Draft Apple privacy labels and Google Play Data safety answers
- The selected Ethiopian bank/payment agreement and API data flow when available

## Ask counsel to deliver

- Final customer Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Returns/Refunds Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Cookie/Tracking disclosure, and copyright/DMCA procedure appropriate to the actual jurisdictions
- Final Seller Agreement and marketplace/merchant-of-record allocation of responsibility
- Approved retention periods and deletion/anonymization rules
- Required consent language and where it must appear in registration, checkout, seller onboarding, and marketing
- Advice on Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, cross-border processing, controller/processor duties, and any registration or representative requirement
- Advice on consumer protection, tax, marketplace, courier, product-safety, sanctions/export, and payment regulations in scope
- A list of operational actions—not just edited documents—and written approval of the final published versions

## Completion steps

1. Resolve every bracketed placeholder and open question with counsel.
2. Update the in-app and web copies from the same approved source text.
3. Enter the approved legal entity/contact values in business configuration.
4. Host the Privacy Policy and support page at stable public HTTPS URLs.
5. Remove every draft/attorney-review banner.
6. Record counsel's final version/date and preserve the approval correspondence.
7. Re-review whenever payment providers, analytics, advertising, countries, data uses, or retention practices change.
